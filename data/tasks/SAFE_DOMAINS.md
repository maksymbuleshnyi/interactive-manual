# Safe domains allow-list (MVP)

Per the "Legal and safety considerations" section of `PLAN.md`. A task may
only be added if it stays inside this allow-list.

## Allowed for the MVP

- Light physical repair / maintenance: windshield wipers, furniture assembly,
  bike basics, replacing batteries.
- Software UI walkthroughs: settings toggles, preference changes, account setup.
- Low-stakes assembly and education tasks.

## Excluded from the MVP (do not author tasks for these)

- Medical or first-aid procedures.
- Electrical work above low voltage.
- Fuel or gas handling.
- Firearms.
- Brake or airbag service.

When in doubt, leave it out and log the question in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
